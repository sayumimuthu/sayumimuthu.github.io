Hi, I'm Sayumi! I am a Research Assistant at the [Multidisciplinary AI Research Centre (MARC)](https://pdnmarc.github.io), University of Peradeniya, where I am the **lead research assistant for the Generative AI – Large Language Model (LLM) research group** and a research assistant in the **Generative AI – Genetics** group. I graduated from the University of Peradeniya in 2025 with a **B.Sc. in Computer Engineering with First Class Honours**.

My research interests are in **natural language processing**, **responsible AI**, **large language models** and **computational biology**, and their applications, mostly in **biomedical engineering**. I am particularly interested in making language models more reliable, fair and safe to use, especially in health-related settings. My current work spans:

- **Trustworthy LLM evaluation:** separating genuine prompt sensitivity from artifacts of heuristic scoring and LLM-as-judge evaluation ([project](/projects/prompt-sensitivity/)).
- **Fairness and social bias:** how gender stereotypes surface in LLM-generated children's stories, and whether model hidden states causally drive them ([project](/projects/gender-stereotypes/)).
- **Hallucination and factuality:** using internal representations and energy-based signals to detect and mitigate hallucinations ([project](/projects/hallucination-detection/)).
- **LLMs in mental health:** whether conversational LLMs recognize depression-related cues, in collaboration with the Department of Psychiatry, University of Illinois Chicago ([project](/projects/depression-cues/)).
- **Generative models for single-cell genomics:** absorbing-state discrete diffusion for scRNA-seq imputation ([project](/projects/absorb/)).

Before joining MARC, I was a research intern at the [NeuroBionics Laboratory](https://biomedical.eng.unimelb.edu.au/john-neurobionics), University of Melbourne, where I contributed to accessible gaming controllers for children with cerebral palsy and explored EEG signals for brain-computer interfaces. My final-year undergraduate research was on [classifying single-joint motor-imagery EEG tasks](/projects/motor-imagery-eeg/). I have also taught undergraduate laboratories and tutorials in the Department of Computer Engineering since 2023.
