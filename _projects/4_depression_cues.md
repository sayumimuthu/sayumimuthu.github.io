**Area:** Mental health AI, NLP, human-AI interaction &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Period:** January 2026 – present &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Collaborators:** Department of Psychiatry, University of Illinois Chicago, and University of Peradeniya

People increasingly turn to general-purpose chatbots during difficult moments. This project investigates whether conversational LLMs recognize **depression-related cues** and respond appropriately to sensitive mental-health disclosures.

#### What I am doing

- Exploring model behavior across **ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and DeepSeek** using **simulated patient conversations**, including direct interactions and agent-assisted settings.
- Collaborating with psychiatrists to develop **clinician-informed evaluation approaches** for cue recognition, conversational appropriateness and differences across models.
- Examining the reliability and safety of LLM responses in mental-health-related conversations, with an emphasis on the limitations of general-purpose AI systems in clinical contexts.

#### Methods

NLP · LLM evaluation · conversational AI · simulated patient interactions · human-AI interaction · responsible AI
