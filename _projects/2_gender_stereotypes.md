**Area:** Responsible AI, natural language processing &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Period:** November 2025 – present &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Status:** Preprint on arXiv {% cite muthukumarana2026onceuponabias %}

LLMs are increasingly used to write stories for children. This project studies whether, and how, those stories encode gender stereotypes, and asks what inside the model drives them.

#### What I did

- Designed **matched daughter-son comparisons** and investigated how the gender of the intended child and the narrator's identity affect character traits and social-role portrayals.
- Built evaluation pipelines combining **agency/communion and trait lexicons**, matched interaction analyses and **human assessments** of narrative differences.
- Examined whether recipient information encoded in model **hidden states** has a causal influence on generated portrayals, using **probing and activation interventions** to distinguish decodability from demonstrated causation.

#### Methods

NLP · algorithmic fairness · quantitative text analysis · human evaluation · representation probing · causal intervention experiments
