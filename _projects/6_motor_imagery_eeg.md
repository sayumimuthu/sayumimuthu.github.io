**Area:** Neuroengineering, brain-computer interfaces &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Period:** January 2025 – present &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Context:** Final-year undergraduate research, Department of Computer Engineering, University of Peradeniya

Most motor-imagery BCIs distinguish between large movements such as left hand vs. right hand. This project tackles a much finer-grained problem: telling apart **imagined wrist pronation and supination** of the same joint, where low signal-to-noise ratios and inter-subject variation make decoding difficult.

#### What I did

- Built EEG pre-processing pipelines with **filtering**, **independent component analysis** and **epoching**.
- Compared **conventional machine-learning** methods with **convolutional**, **recurrent** and **Transformer-based** models.

#### Methods

EEG signal processing · motor imagery · brain-computer interfaces · representation learning · model evaluation
