**Area:** Trustworthy LLMs, factuality &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Period:** October 2026 – present &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Related:** Systematic literature review under review at _Computational Linguistics_ {% cite muthukumarana2026hallucinationslr %}

A single model-confidence score is often a poor indicator of whether a generated claim is true. This project develops a research framework that assesses the factual reliability of generated claims through **multiple energy signals**.

#### What I did

- Contributed to a research framework for assessing the factual reliability of generated claims through multiple energy signals rather than a single model-confidence measure.
- Evaluated research directions involving **semantic equivalence**, **natural language inference**, **claim-level verification** and **calibration**.
- Led a **systematic literature review** of methods that use LLM internal representations for hallucination detection and mitigation.

#### Methods

Energy-based learning · NLI · semantic entropy probes · factuality verification · uncertainty estimation · contrastive evaluation
